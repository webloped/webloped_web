The workshop

Watch a website take shape.

Four stages, one build — strategy becomes structure, design, a working interface, and the automation that follows up while you sleep. Scroll to assemble it, or use the controls.

Stage 1 · Strategy

Structure and hierarchy.

We decide what the site must say and in what order — the pages, the sections, and the single action each one exists to earn. Nothing decorative survives this stage.

Stage 2 · Design

Typography, imagery, and colour.

Real words set in real type, a palette with a point of view, and imagery chosen for the business. This is where a website starts to feel inevitable.

Stage 3 · Development

A polished desktop and mobile interface.

The design becomes a fast, responsive website — as considered on a phone in one hand as on a studio monitor.

Stage 4 · Automation

Enquiry, confirmation, and follow-up.

Every enquiry is acknowledged in seconds and followed up without chasing. The site keeps working after the visitor leaves — that is the part most websites never get.

Process

A clear path from first conversation to launch.

No black box, no big reveal. You always know where your project stands and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We start with a call about your business: what you offer, who it is for, and what a good website would change. You will know quickly whether we are the right fit — and we will tell you honestly if we are not.

  2. 02

    Design & build

    We design in the open, in rounds you can react to, then build the approved design as a fast, responsive website. You see real progress throughout — never a surprise unveiling.

  3. 03

    Launch & after

    We launch carefully and check everything twice. The site is yours, outright — and if you would like us to keep improving it, we stay within reach.
