About

A small senior team in Cambridge, Ontario.

Webloped designs and builds websites, then wires in AI automation so the site keeps working after launch. No account managers, no hand-offs - you talk to the people doing the work.

How we work

Built like a craft, run like a business.

Most small businesses get their website from one of two places: a template they outgrow in a year, or an agency process built for clients ten times their size. Webloped sits in the middle - custom-built sites and practical automation, delivered by a senior team small enough that nothing gets lost between the person you talk to and the person who builds.

We start every project the same way: understanding what the website needs to achieve for the business. Then we design, build, and test it like something with our name on it - because it has our name on it.

After launch, we stay optional but available. Care plans cover the domain, hosting, and everyday edits for businesses that would rather not think about their website. If you would rather run it yourself, every project includes handover documentation - no lock-in, no hostage clauses.
