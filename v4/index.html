Questions

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — pages, e-commerce, integrations, and automation. Tell us about your project and we'll respond with a fixed quote in CAD that itemizes exactly what's included, plus any recurring costs like hosting or care plans.

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you receive the finished site, its content, and documentation. We don't hold your business hostage to keep you paying.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance?

Yes — we can set up hosting and offer care plans covering updates, backups, security, and small changes. If you'd rather manage it yourself, we'll hand everything over cleanly.

Is SEO included?

SEO foundations are: fast loads, mobile-first builds, proper page structure, and local business setup. What we won't do is promise rankings — anyone guaranteeing a #1 spot is selling you something.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Absolutely — redesigns are some of our favourite projects. We audit what you have, keep what works (including your existing search visibility), and rebuild the rest.

How do we start?

Send us a few lines about your project through the form below, email us directly, or book a 30-minute call. We'll reply with next steps and a fixed quote.

Where are you located?

Cambridge, Ontario, Canada — reachable at contact@webloped.ca or +1 (647) 694-7001. Most projects run fully remote; if you're nearby, we're happy to meet in person.
