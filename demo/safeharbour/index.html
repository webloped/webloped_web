Know what you’re dealing with

Recognising abuse

  • Controlling who you see, where you go, or what you wear
  • Constant criticism, humiliation, or threats
  • Monitoring your phone, messages, or location
  • Isolating you from friends and family
  • Any physical harm, or threats of harm

Signs of a healthy relationship

  • You feel safe to say no
  • Disagreements happen without fear
  • Your boundaries are respected
  • You stay connected to your own friends and family
  • Support goes both ways

Your rights in Ontario

  • You have the right to live free from violence and threats
  • You can call 911 in an emergency — no permission needed
  • Shelters cannot turn you away for lack of money
  • Free legal advice is available through Legal Aid Ontario
  • Your immigration status does not remove your right to safety
