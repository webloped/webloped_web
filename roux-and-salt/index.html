01 · Our Story

Meet the chef behind Roux & Salt

Roux & Salt is the creation of Renier Schoeman, an Executive Chef whose career has been shaped in some of the world's finest kitchens, with international luxury hospitality experience and five-star hotel expertise at its heart.

His philosophy is simple: food should be exceptional and comforting at once. Refined technique, quality ingredients and genuine creativity come together in dishes that are as memorable as they are approachable, crafted for the moments that matter, from intimate dinners at home to celebrations shared with hundreds.

Every menu is personal. Every plate is a small act of hospitality. That is what "Crafted with Love" means at Roux & Salt.

  • ExperienceInternational luxury hospitality & five-star hotels
  • PhilosophyRefined yet approachable cuisine

02 · Catering & Events

Occasions, beautifully catered

From boardroom lunches to candlelit private dinners, every Roux & Salt event is bespoke. Menus are designed around your guests, your setting and the season, then executed with five-star precision.

No. 01

Corporate Catering

Polished catering for meetings, launches and business functions, from working lunches to full reception service, delivered seamlessly to your office or venue.

No. 02

Private Dining

An intimate chef-led dining experience in your home or chosen setting. Multi-course tasting menus, plated with intention, served with warmth.

No. 03

High Tea & Gatherings

Elegant afternoon teas and refined gatherings, with delicate pastries, finger sandwiches, scones and fine teas presented in classic style.

No. 04

Canapés & Hors d'oeuvres

Bite-sized creations for receptions and cocktail occasions, designed to be as beautiful as they are delicious, and effortless for guests to enjoy.

No. 05

Family-Style Celebrations

Generous shared platters for birthdays, anniversaries and family milestones. Abundant, comforting food that brings everyone to the table.

No. 06

Seasonal & Holiday

Festive menus for the holidays and special seasons, from elegant Christmas gatherings to summer garden celebrations, crafted around the best of the moment.

Growing toward a café & gourmet destination

Roux & Salt is evolving from a chef-led culinary business into a recognizable gourmet food and hospitality brand. Our long-term vision is a café and gourmet retail concept, a place where fresh food, artisan products and an elevated culinary experience meet.
