The practice

Est. Toronto

Atelier North is a small architecture studio in Toronto. We take on a few projects at a time — houses, workplaces, and quiet public spaces — and stay with each one from first sketch to final walkthrough.

Our work starts with listening: how you live, how you work, how light moves through your day. The drawings come later. We believe the best buildings are the ones that feel like they could not have been any other way.

“We design for the Tuesday morning, not the magazine shoot — the everyday moments a building has to get right.”
Mara Ellingsen, Founding Principal
