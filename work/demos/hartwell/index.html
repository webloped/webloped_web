What we build

Three trades we know inside out.

We keep our focus narrow on purpose. Fewer service lines means every carpenter, tile setter, and electrician on your job does this work every single week.

Kitchens

A kitchen renovation touches plumbing, electrical, cabinetry, and stone — all at once. We sequence every trade so the room is never sitting idle, and we mock up the layout full-scale before a single cabinet is ordered.

You get one fixed quote, a written schedule, and a project lead who answers the phone.

  • Custom cabinetry
  • Stone & tile
  • Layout redesign

Bathrooms

Small rooms punish sloppy work. We rebuild bathrooms from the studs out — proper waterproofing, level tile, and ventilation sized for the room — so the finish you see on day one still looks right in year ten.

Most full bathrooms complete in three to four weeks, ensuite to powder room.

  • Walk-in showers
  • Heated floors
  • Vanities & fixtures

Home additions

When the house is right but the space is wrong, an addition beats a move. We handle drawings, permits, foundation, framing, and the interior finish — blending the new square footage so it reads as original to the home.

Permit paperwork is on us, and we keep you living comfortably in the house while we build around you.

  • Second storeys
  • Rear extensions
  • In-law suites

How we work
