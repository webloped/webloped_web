Concept project

Hartwell & Sons

A complete concept website for a fictional premium home-renovation company in Cambridge, Ontario — built to show how a trades business earns trust before the first phone call.

Concept website: design, copy, and build
Status
Original demonstration — not a real client

What the concept includes

A full single-page site built around the one thing a renovation company needs: the estimate request. Services (kitchens, bathrooms, additions), a plain-spoken process, service area, and a contact section with an estimate-request form.

The copy is written to defuse the industry's trust problem head-on: one dedicated project lead, no hand-offs, no surprises. Contact details shown are illustrative placeholders.

Design notes

Trust is the product

Renovation is a high-trust purchase. The design answers the visitor's real questions — who shows up, what it costs, what happens when — before asking for anything.

One job per screen

Every section ends at the same action: request an estimate. Repetition with restraint turns browsing into booking.

Crafted, not corporate

Forest green and brass signal craft and heritage; the typography stays warm and plain-spoken, like the company it imagines.
