Concept project

Atelier North

A complete concept website for a fictional Toronto architecture practice — designed to show how a quiet, confident studio presents its work online.

Imagined client
Atelier North — architecture studio, Toronto
What we made
Concept website: design, copy, and build
Status
Original demonstration — not a real client

What the concept includes

A full single-page site with everything a small practice needs: an editorial hero, a works index with project detail, the studio's story, services, and a contact section with an enquiry form.

The design language is deliberately restrained — generous whitespace, a refined serif for voice, architectural line drawings instead of stock photography, and one bronze accent used sparingly.

Open the live concept ↗

Design notes

Typography carries it

A high-contrast serif headline against quiet sans body copy gives the studio an editorial voice — no decoration required.

Drawn, not decorated

Architectural line drawings stand in for photography, keeping the concept honest about what it is while showing real art direction.

One accent, used rarely

Bronze appears only where it earns attention: the wordmark's second word, key rules, and the primary action.
